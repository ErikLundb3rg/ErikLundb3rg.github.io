---
title: Top 7 Addis Ababa Meals
---

In this list you find the top 7 meals I had while I was a TA at [AddisCoder](https://www.addiscoder.com/) in Ethiopia, 2026.

1. Hand pulled noodles at [Lanzhou Beef Noodles](https://maps.app.goo.gl/198sMAABiict48qE6).
The presence of Chinese nationals is reassuring. Good portion and nice side dish. Fair price.
2. Tere Siga at [Shebeta](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tgkV8BYagC2EQSqX9). Raw meat directly from the carcass served with sauces. Great stuff -- I did not get a tapeworm.
3. Japchae, Fried chicken, Tteokbokki and more select items at [Arirang](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LLJcGbTGoXUzUbEY9). Authentic korean food. The bbq meats are alright, although a bit overpriced. One of few places where you can eat pork in Addis.
4. Lamb tibs at [Azmera](https://maps.app.goo.gl/SSsuHWgQU66fqhUU8). Unlike at other places, this tibs is tender. Great portion and price. Place is packed for a reason.
5. Spaghetti Bolognese at [this](https://maps.app.goo.gl/j65ReBcSMCfRE9if8) location in Entoto park. Having a real, well cooked pasta which is not filled with green chillies is a blessing after weeks of injera.
7. Yemeni food at [Bab Al-Yemen](https://maps.app.goo.gl/w1RFZE2Tbg5RhyqB8). All the food is prepared fresh. Expect fragrant rice, fresh flatbread, tender lamb/chicken. The tahini and mango lassi are great as well..
8. Butter chicken combo at [Chillies](https://maps.app.goo.gl/GUH76rycKrjY4FFb7). An absolute indian feast. The butter chicken here is spicy unlike the traditional variant. Pair it with a Mango Lassi.
